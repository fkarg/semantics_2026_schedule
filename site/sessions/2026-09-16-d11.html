Agentic AI: May the Knowledge Graph Be with You!

Fishbowl

Scheduled: September 16, from 5 to 6pm, in room Kabinet

Agentic AI needs Knowledge Graphs and vice versa – Discussion of Pros & Cons

This session will bring together two first-class citizens in the future AI ecosystem: AI Agents and Knowledge Graphs (KG). Statistics and Semantics. Educated guesses and educated statements. Both need each other and both profit from each other. Or is this wishful thinking of the semantic web community? The AI community might argue that creating a statistically sound guessing system based on factual data is better than trusting random knowledge collected by some humans... Let’s discuss!

The open discussion takes place in a fishbowl format. In this format everyone is invited to join the “panel” - that is like a fishbowl, set-up in the center of the room. We expect experts from the audience to kick-off the discussion and continue along our common thread.

We will steer the discussion around clear benefits for both worlds with questions like:

  • ​How can Semantics help Agentic AI with its quality ceiling gap challenge?
  • How can semantic technologies, how can KG’s help to trust the results of Agent AI models?
  • How can KGs help to maintain ownership of knowledge?
  • How can Agentic AI help to build and maintain Knowledge Graphs fast and with limited (human) resources?
  • How could an integrated semantic agentic AI pipeline look like that leverages the capabilities of both technologies?

As a wrap-up we will summarize the results together, identifying some major opportunities and risks. The more concrete requirements, use cases and solution strategies we gather from the participating audience, the higher the impact will be for further discussions on the second day of SEMANTiCS conference and further on.
