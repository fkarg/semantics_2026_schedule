Neurosymbolic AI and Human-Centered Explainability: from Neuron Activations to Explanations and Back

Alessandra Mileo (Dublin City University)

Neuro-Symbolic AI is becoming a fast-growing area of research. However, there is still a lot of potential for leveraging neuro-symbolic approaches to address the need for explainability and confidence. These are key requirements when it comes to using neural models in computer vision and language processing to support human experts in high-stake decision making. In this talk I will set the scene with preliminaries on Explainability and Interpretability, and cover approaches for understanding deep representations via concept-neuron association, co-activations analysis and knowledge graphs, and their applicability in real-world scenarios.

I will also discuss recent advances in the design of a neurosymbolic cycle for Human-centered explainability by identifying the key elements I believe are needed to create a full cycle from neurons' activation to human-centered explanations and back to model adaptation. We will discuss opportunities offered by Neuro-Symbolic integration, and open challenges in the design of such a cycle.
