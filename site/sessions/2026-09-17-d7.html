Thursday · 17 September · Session 4.3 (SEMANTiCS)

Cool Solutions Meet Tricky Problems (A marketplace of ideas and questions)

13:00 – 14:40 CEST · Kabinet

Chair: Marco Brattinga, Christian Dirschl, Frank van Harmelen and Ute John

Cool Solutions Meet Tricky Problems

A marketplace of ideas and challenges

Scheduled: September 17, from 1pm to 2.30pm, in room Kabinet

Are you aware that there is a format during SEMANTiCS Conference that allows you to jump in and discuss spontaneously with fellow experts both from industry and academia about a pressing problem you are facing, or, about the real world relevance of a solution you have worked on for so long already?

Come and join our interactive session “Cool Solutions Meet Tricky Problems”.

This networking event follows a mini bar camp approach with a series of discussions in parallel break-out groups. Everybody joining is invited to bring his or her problem/approach to the table so that the break-out group may work on it collaboratively.

In these break-out dialogues you may contribute a pressing problem and ask for solution ideas. Or, you could sketch or demo your innovative idea and evaluate its relevance and usability.

If you want to prepare upfront, please follow these question lines: * “What practical challenge are you currently facing and do you want to discuss?” * “What idea/solution do you currently have available that you would like to check for its practical value and validity?”

As a wrap-up the results of the discussions will be summarized at the end of the session.

We look forward to welcome you at SEMANTiCS conference on the 16th of September at 17.

Marco Brattinga, Christian Dirschl, Frank van Harmelen and Ute John

Supported by GfWM (Gesellschaft für Wissensmanagement e. V.)
