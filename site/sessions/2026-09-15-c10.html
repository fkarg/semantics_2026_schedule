DBpedia 2026: Creating Opportunities for the DBpedia Community

What could a more community-driven future for DBpedia look like? This session invites DBpedia contributors, chapter leads, organisations and community members to discuss how we can work together to address long-standing challenges and further develop DBpedia.

The discussion will explore a possible community model built around four key elements: a shared Knowledge Graph Lifecycle System (KGLS) and interoperable services; DBpedia Community Projects such as language, thematic and regional initiatives; shared infrastructure and operations; and new roles and opportunities for community members and organisations to contribute, lead projects and support the DBpedia ecosystem.

The session is intended as an open discussion to gather perspectives, ideas and concrete opportunities for collaboration and to jointly shape the future of the DBpedia community.
