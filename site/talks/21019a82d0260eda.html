Abstract

Semantic technologies are widely recognised as a means to achieve interoperability in heterogeneous digital ecosystems. However, many ontology-driven initiatives remain limited to conceptual modelling activities and fail to demonstrate how semantic models can be integrated into operational data exchange infrastructures. This work presents the Semantic Resource Interface (SRI), an interoperability component developed within the Horizon Europe RESONANCE project to enable semantic integration across buildings, energy management systems, electric vehicle charging infrastructure, IoT devices, and flexibility services. In industrial environments, these systems operate in isolated silos, having heterogeneous data models, APIs, naming conventions, and various interpretations of identical concepts, which makes semantic interoperability both challenging and costly. Beyond integration at the data level, a key challenge is enabling a continuous bridge between heterogeneous but non-homogenised data sources and downstream energy applications and services. To overcome this challenge at an operational level, we designed the RESONANCE ontology that contains five ontology modules that reuse existing standard energy domain ontologies like SAREF (The Smart Applications REFerence Ontology) and its extensions SAREF4ENER and SAREF4BLDG. Then we implemented a shared, common semantic interoperability component, the Semantic Resource Interface (SRI).
