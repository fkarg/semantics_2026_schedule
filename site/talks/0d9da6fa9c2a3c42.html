Automated legal reasoning system for generating draft responses to claims, based on Neurosymbolic AI on knowledge graphs.

Ricardo Alonso-Maturana, Angel Esteban Moreno-Beltrán, Marcos Campazas, Susana López-Sola and Karmele Alonso Aia

10:30 – 12:00 CEST · Kraakhuis

Abstract

If this case is selected, the final case presentation is subject to approval by our client. An insurance entity manages thousands of lawsuits annually across two highly complex regulatory domains: extraordinary risks (floods, earthquakes, terrorist attacks) and compulsory motor vehicle insurance. Each case file arrives as heterogeneous documentary chaos: procedural briefs, expert reports, photographs, scanned administrative files. Processing it through traditional methods represents weeks of specialized legal work, with the risk of argumentative inconsistency and exposure to deadlines that the judicial system does not forgive. GNOSS has built and deployed an automated legal reasoning system that does not merely retrieve relevant documents, but reasons over them. Its core is a knowledge graph encoding the regulatory pyramid applicable to the fund — from the Constitution and EU law down to administrative circulars — integrated with a jurisprudential layer of five thousand CENDOJ rulings. The system extracts the legally material entities from the case file, classifies the case, traverses the corresponding argumentative chain, and identifies the optimal procedural strategy from twelve predefined options. Each inference is an examinable logical instance, not a statistical prediction. The result is a tool that complies by design with the explainability and auditability requirements that the European AI Act demands of high-risk systems. The lawyer does not receive an answer: they receive a line of reasoning they can follow, challenge, and sign. What previously required days of work is completed in minutes, with full traceability and systematic argumentative consistency.
