Abstract

The web was built on open standards — and so should your software. At redpencil, we build modular, open source applications grounded in W3C standards like RDF, SPARQL, LDES and RDFa. These are not just technical choices: they are architectural decisions that guarantee interoperability, longevity and freedom from vendor lock-in.We bridge the gap between research-grade technology and real-world business problems. Our applications are built to be user-friendly — the semantic complexity stays under the hood, invisible to end users, while delivering data that is meaningful, traceable and reusable by design. We have proven this in production — from local government decision platforms and region-wide data registrations to the central decision-making infrastructure of the Flemish Government.This talk shows how open standards, modular architecture and open source combine into a sovereign, scalable alternative to the monolithic platforms that dominate today's digital landscape — and why that matters more than ever in a world where technological independence has become a strategic necessity.
