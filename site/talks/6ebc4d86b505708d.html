Thursday · 17 September · Conference Keynote

From GOFAI to NewFAI - From Good Old Fashioned AI to New Fashioned AI

Deborah McGuinness (Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute)

09:00 – 10:00 CEST · Concertzaal

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Angelo Salatino

Online room

Abstract

Artificial intelligence has evolved from foundations grounded in explicit knowledge representation, formal semantics, and symbolic reasoning to a new AI landscape increasingly influenced—and sometimes dominated—by large language models, agents, and rapidly evolving generative technologies. Drawing on my perspective as a longtime ontology and semantics researcher and professor, I will discuss approaches that aim to leverage the strengths of both worlds. I will present emerging hybrid architectures that integrate ontologies and semantic technologies with LLM-based pipelines, illustrating how these approaches are reshaping several of my current research projects. I will also explore how this transition is transforming AI education. In particular, I will describe a new model for readings courses in fast-moving fields that I developed and used in my course on knowledge-graph-powered hybrid AI. I will then discuss exploratory approaches to incorporating generative and agentic AI into my ontology engineering course, including the design and classroom integration of an agentic teaching assistant. This assistant is intended to support students while reinforcing—not replacing—the critical thinking, conceptual modeling, and semantic discipline required for effective ontology engineering. Together, these experiences offer a practical view of how researchers and educators can connect AI’s foundations to its rapidly unfolding future.
