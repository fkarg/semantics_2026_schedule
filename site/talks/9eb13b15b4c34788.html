More Than Data: Making Knowledge Graphs Work Together for Actionable Insights

Prof. Dr. Ricardo Usbeck

09:15 – 10:00 CEST · Concertzaal

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Sebastian Hellmann

Online room

Abstract

While large language models (LLMs) have driven remarkable progress in natural language processing, their application in high-stakes, dynamic environments remains constrained by limited factual grounding, interpretability, and user trust. To address these fundamental challenges, our research group (https://www.leuphana.de/aix) investigates the neuro-symbolic integration of state-of-the-art language models with a multitude of dynamic knowledge graphs. By anchoring generative architectures in structured, continuously updated real-world facts, we enable AI systems to predict supply chain reliability or the trajectories of natural disasters. This keynote will highlight our recent contributions to the deployment of domain-specific, autonomous AI agents for crisis management and supply chain resilience. Drawing on insights from the COYPU, PROVIDER, and RescueMate projects - where robust, real-time event extraction from heterogeneous global data streams is critical - this talk will show how only a collection of integrated Knowledge Graphs can provide the verifiable ground truth necessary to deploy autonomous AI in environments where failure is not an option.
