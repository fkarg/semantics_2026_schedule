Semantica: A Context and Accountability Layer for Knowledge Graph-Powered AI System (online)

Mohammad Kaif, Semantica

10:20 – 11:50 CEST · Kraakhuis

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Milan Dojchinovski

Online room

Abstract

Knowledge Graphs have become an essential component of modern AI systems, yet most LLM-based applications still rely on vector stores and short-term memory mechanisms that provide limited explainability, provenance, and accountability. As AI systems move into production, particularly in regulated domains, there is a growing need for systems that can answer not only what was generated, but also why a decision was made and where the underlying evidence originated.

In this talk, we present Semantica, an open-source framework that introduces Context Graphs as a persistent intelligence layer for AI systems. Semantica combines knowledge graph construction, provenance tracking, deterministic reasoning, ontology management, and temporal intelligence in a single platform. The framework supports the complete lifecycle of knowledge graphs, from multi-source ingestion and information extraction to entity resolution, reasoning, governance, and interactive exploration.

We will demonstrate how Context Graphs extend traditional Knowledge Graphs by treating decisions, evidence, and provenance as first-class graph entities, enabling explainable AI, auditable decision histories, and richer GraphRAG workflows. We also discuss practical applications in regulatory intelligence, compliance, and enterprise AI systems, and explore opportunities for integrating provenance-aware and reasoning-centric capabilities into the broader DBpedia ecosystem.
