Tuesday · 15 September · DBpedia

An Explainable Hybrid GraphRAG Framework for Diabetes Risk Stratification (online)

Herman Kandolo, Vietnam National University & La Rochelle Université

12:40 – 14:10 CEST · Kraakhuis

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Milan Dojchinovski

Online room

Abstract

Large Language Models (LLMs) have significantly improved the usability of clinical decision support systems, but they still suffer from high context consumption and limited explainability when reasoning over medical data. We present an explainable Hybrid GraphRAG framework that shifts statistical reasoning from the LLM to a deterministic knowledge graph, allowing the language model to focus exclusively on generating clinician-friendly explanations. The framework combines unsupervised learning, supervised classification, Neo4j-based knowledge graphs, and LLM-based natural language generation into a unified decision-support pipeline.

The system builds epidemiological knowledge from the BRFSS 2015 dataset through K-Means clustering and stores the resulting profiles in a Neo4j knowledge graph. During online inference, a new patient is assigned to the most appropriate epidemiological profile using a K-NN classifier. The corresponding statistical evidence is retrieved through Cypher queries and provided to the LLM as a compact and fully auditable context for explanation generation. Experimental results demonstrate a 97.31% reduction in LLM context size compared with a vector-based RAG baseline while eliminating numerical hallucinations in our evaluation and providing fully traceable decision evidence. The presentation will demonstrate the complete workflow of the prototype and discuss how deterministic knowledge graphs can improve transparency and trust in GraphRAG-based clinical decision support systems.
