Thursday · 17 September · Industry and Use Cases

Creating an AI-ready fundament for the Dutch Digital System for the Environment and Planning Act

Jan Lyckle Bijlsma and Art Ligthart

10:30 – 12:00 CEST · Kraakhuis

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Beatriz Gonçalves Crisóstomo Esteves

Online room

Abstract

AI applications for the Dutch Digital System for the Environment and Planning Act (DSO: Digitaal Stelsel Omgevingswet) increasingly support citizens and professionals in interpreting environmental and planning regulations. However, these applications operate in a legally sensitive domain in which answer quality must not only be useful, but also correct, traceable, and explainable. This creates a need for a semantic infrastructure that can bridge user language and legal concepts, connect the concepts between different domains and create a fundament for AI applications to build on. Our work combines semantic modelling of DSO data with graph-based modelling of legal texts from national, provincial, municipal, and water authority sources. The graph links user-facing activities, legal activities, legal rules, concepts, and legislative structure. To support legal traceability, statutes and decrees were transformed from XML into RDF graph structures using a dedicated ontology in combination with the ontology of European Legislation Identifier (ELI). A graph agent then decomposes a user question into key terms and user-facing activities. The agent then traverses through the graph to retrieve relevant legal context, and re-ranks candidate documents. This approach allows the modelling of knowledge graphs to be directly used as input for (LLM-based) AI-solutions, resulting in a clearer overview of used sources and provides visualisation potential for increased trust and understanding from the user’s perspective.
