From Linked Data to AI-Ready Data: Five Floors, and How to Build Them [SP]

Agis Papantoniou (Cognizone)

10:30 – 12:00 CEST · Kraakhuis

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Beatriz Gonçalves Crisóstomo Esteves

Online room

Abstract

Every organisation now says it wants its data to be "AI-ready". Almost nobody can say what that means, or how to get there - and the usual answer, put the documents in a vector database and connect an LLM, produces impressive demos that stall before production. The model is rarely the problem. The data underneath it is.This talk introduces a practical framework for AI-ready data: five floors, built foundation-first, in the spirit of Tim Berners-Lee's five-star model for linked open data. Each floor is a state your data is either in or not, testable by specific conditions that can be checked in a pragmatic way.Most of the session focuses on how the floors get built. For every new floor we discuss what it provides, how to tell whether you have successfully built it, and what it takes to reach the next one.This is based on a decade of experience building production knowledge graphs for EU institutions and regulated industries.
