Abstract

The Knowledge Graph ecosystem is rapidly growing, but discovering, understanding, and reusing Knowledge Graphs remains challenging: Where are relevant Knowledge Graphs? What do they contain? Which versions are available, and where can the data be accessed?

This talk introduces the DBpedia Knowledge Graph Catalog, an initiative for discovering, describing, and reusing Knowledge Graphs. While the Linked Open Data Cloud focuses on datasets, links, and the broader ecosystem, the KG Catalog takes a complementary perspective centred on Knowledge Graphs, their metadata, releases, and artifacts. Versioning, access, and machine-readable metadata are treated as first-class elements, enabling not only discovery but also operational reuse.

The talk presents the motivation and concepts behind the KG Catalog, demonstrates how Knowledge Graphs can be described and accessed through the catalog, and provides a live demonstration using real-world examples. Finally, it introduces the broader vision of building a comprehensive Knowledge Graph landscape and invites the community to contribute by cataloging Knowledge Graphs, curating metadata, and building services on top of the catalog.

Please join the KG Catalog Initiative: https://kg-catalog.dbpedia.org.
