Abstract

This talk explores the critical shift from viewing semantics as a purely technical exercise to understanding it as the foundation of shared meaning within an enterprise. By examining architectural patterns, common anti-patterns, and essential governance practices, the session outlines the Enterprise Semantic Design Pyramid and explains why successful initiatives must prioritize business strategy over technology. Attendees will gain a framework for navigating semantic challenges through established design principles and a maturity model — ultimately learning how to transform semantics from a technical feature into a core business capability that enables truly meaningful and autonomous AI.
