AI-Driven Ontology Development for Multi-Lingual and Multi-Jurisdiction Financial Services - the HYPOPASS case

Leon Roseleur

10:30 – 12:00 CEST · Concertzaal

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Harshvardhan Pandit

Online room

Abstract

Creating a data standard across multiple jurisdictions isn't a technology problem. The real challenge lays in the fact that the same concept often has a different meaning depending on the legal system, language, or sector in which it is used. Standardizing on the technical layer does not solve those differences, unless organizations connect on both the semantic, the legal and the process-level. Otherwise, interoperability remains limited. We refer to this challenge as the legal-semantic gap. A good example is HypoPass, a Belgian-Dutch mortgage industry initiative that connects 16 banks and thousands of financial advisors across Belgium. The initiative spans three languages (Dutch, French and English), brings together 14 different data models and handles different jurisdictions across Wallonia, Flanders and Brussels. We were asked to develop interoperability between all parties involved. The complete data exchange consisted of approximately 4,800 definitions. Using conventional methods, this exercise was expected to take around 18 months. By applying an AI-supported methodology aligned with the European Interoperability Framework (EIF), we delivered a validated ontology within 13 weeks while maintaining an error rate below 0.5% - connecting the legal, semantic, organizational and layer. A key design principle is that experts remain in control throughout the process. AI performs the initial analysis and proposes mappings, while legal, business and domain specialists validate the results before moving to the next stage. This approach reduces the effort required per concept from hours to minutes and shortens overall delivery timelines tremendously. After this initial project, the same method tested and applied within various use cases spanning from Tax Office, Customs, Life Sciences and company law.
