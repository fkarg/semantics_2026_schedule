SEOntology: A Domain Ontology for Semantic Modeling of Search Engine Optimization Workflows 🎖️

Emilija Gjorgjevska, David Riccitelli, Milos Jovanovik and Andrea Volpini

10:30 – 12:00 CEST · Concertzaal

Session time; individual talk times are not specified in the timetable.

Chair: Harshvardhan Pandit

Online room

Abstract

SEO workflows remain difficult to automate and integrate because they span heterogeneous tools while relying on loosely structured data models that provide limited machine-interpretable representations of content, actions, and performance signals. This fragmentation hinders interoperability and makes provenance tracking across SEO systems brittle. We present SEOntology, a Schema.org-aligned OWL 2 ontology that models SEO workflows as knowledge-graph structures to support structured querying and provenance-aware analysis. The ontology enables queries for content-quality inspection, entity-coverage analysis, and end-to-end tracing of analytical actions through their inputs, outputs, and generated signals. We assess SEOntology via structural ontology evaluation and competency-question validation, and report supporting evidence from production deployment. These results indicate logical consistency, coverage of the defined requirements, and practical utility as a semantic integration layer for representing and querying SEO workflows. SEOntology is designed as a reusable semantic resource for knowledge-graph–driven and agent-assisted SEO systems, and is extensible toward emerging generative and AI-mediated search environments.
